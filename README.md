In the era of advanced data collection technologies, protecting personal privacy has become increasingly important. Local Differential Privacy (LDP) is an effective method for privacy preservation and is widely used in academia and industry. This paper concentrates on LDP's perturbation mechanisms, which involve encoding, perturbation, and aggregation. Unlike traditional mechanisms based on sampling with replacement, our research investigates mechanisms under a sampling without replacement scheme, offering more precise variance analysis and efficient LDP mechanisms for analyzing entire populations. The Improved Simmons mechanism yields the lowest variance below a certain privacy budget threshold, whereas Improved Warner is superior above this threshold. These findings are consistent with practical scenarios. In real-world datasets, Improved Warner mechanisms reduce variance to 28.7\% and Improved Simmons to 11.7\% of their original levels. We also present a practical example of deploying these mechanisms in real-world settings. Our source code, data, and supplementary materials are available at \url{https://github.com/ssjhf/LDP-Perturbation-Mechanisms.git}
